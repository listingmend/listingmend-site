Google listing suspended in Oklahoma City or Lawton? What to do

Not affiliated with Google. General information, not a guarantee. Google makes every decision about your Business Profile.

If your Google Business Profile was suspended, or Google keeps asking you to verify, the fix is the same in Oklahoma City, Lawton, Tulsa, or anywhere else: find out exactly what Google flagged, fix it at the source, then appeal once with documents that match. Here is the short version, based on Google's own help pages.

Do these first

  1. Open Google's appeals tool before you change anything. It shows the decision, the reason, and whether the profile can be appealed.
  2. Don't make a new profile. Google says not to create a second Business Profile for the same business. A fresh profile usually makes things worse.
  3. Appeal only once you're ready. Google says an appeal can only be submitted once, so make your fixes and gather your documents first.

Problems that come up often for Oklahoma service businesses

  • One business, two cities. If you work in both the Oklahoma City metro and Lawton, that is still one business and one profile, unless you have a staffed location in each. A second profile with a mailing address in the other city is the kind of thing Google suspends.
  • Working from home. If you go to customers and don't serve them at your address, Google says to clear the address from your profile and show a service area instead.
  • Service area too big. Google says a service area shouldn't extend farther than about 2 hours of driving from where you're based, with exceptions for some businesses. A metro contractor listing the whole state is a common flag.
  • Virtual offices and P.O. boxes. Google doesn't accept a P.O. box or a rented mailing address you don't work from.
  • Extra words in the name. "Best OKC Plumbing & Drain 24/7" is not a business name. Use the name on your sign, truck, and paperwork.
  • Documents that don't match. Your registration, license, or utility bills should show the same name and address as your profile.
