Privacy Note

Effective date: October 20, 2026

What we collect: your name and contact details; your business profile information (name, address or service area, hours, services, photos); and the support emails you send us.

Why: only to set up and maintain your listings, and to answer your questions. As part of the service, we publish the business details you approve on Google, Apple Business Connect, Bing, or Yelp.

We never sell your information. We never ask for passwords, or card or bank numbers, by email; you give us manager access instead.

Payments: Payments are handled by Square. We never see or store your full card number.

Service providers: we use email, website form, and AI support tools only to provide the service. Support emails may be processed by an AI assistant, Mend. Reply "human" anytime to reach a person. How long we keep it: support emails are kept for 24 months, then deleted. Signed agreements, invoices, and payment records are kept for 7 years for tax records, then deleted.

Your choices: to see, correct, or delete your information, email help@listingmend.com.

Children: our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes: if this note changes, we'll update the effective date above.
