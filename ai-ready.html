What I do

  1. Test what AI says about you today. I ask the AI assistants people use most the same three questions about your business: who to hire, what it costs, how to book. Today that means ChatGPT and Google's AI (the Gemini app and AI answers in Google Search), plus others such as Copilot or Perplexity when they matter in your market.
  2. Tune up your Google Business Profile. Categories, services, hours (holiday hours too), service area, booking details, Q&A and photos.
  3. Make your website easy for AI to read. Structured business information, a plain facts page (services, starting prices, hours, area, how to book), and settings that let AI tools read it.
  4. Match your facts everywhere. Name, address, phone and hours the same on Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and Facebook.
  5. Re-test after 14 days. You get a before-and-after table showing what each AI assistant said.
